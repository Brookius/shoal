One thing the bubbles needed that isn't a token. The caustics shimmer on .stat-card is specified as a multiply-blended warm layer, which on a light card pools light and improves contrast with the figure. Multiply against a dark card only removes what little light is there, so the effect disappears and the bubble flattens. Rendered above as an additive cool wash instead — same intent (dappled light on a data surface), inverted operation. Flagged as a texture-layer decision for implementation, not a colour-token one: it's the third instance of the same pattern this pass found, after .iucn-CR's fill and --profile-deco.
