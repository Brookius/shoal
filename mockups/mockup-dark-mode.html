Dark mode — three palette directions

Each is an extension of the existing palette, not an inversion. Shared logic across all three: the cream paper becomes the ink (text); the accent splits into accent-fill (buttons — unchanged #4A90B8 + white, identical in both modes) and accent-ink (a lifted cerulean for text/strokes so interactive elements don’t sink into dark backgrounds); IUCN keeps its hue identity but CR holds rank through weight + stronger fill rather than darkness. Only the base canvas differs between the three options.

A · Harbour Night — the sidebar becomes the world
The palette already contains its own dark pole: the #1C3030 sidebar teal. A deepens it into the canvas — the most “native” extension, ink↔paper swapped. Warm taupe survives as the muted text ramp, keeping the brand’s warmth. Sidebar separates from the canvas by dropping one step darker.

B · Dusk Deck — sand at night
Keeps the brand’s warmth: a charcoal-brown canvas derived from the taupe/sand axis, like the boat deck after sunset. The cool video stage + cerulean accents pop hardest here (cool screen in a warm room — the cinema effect). The most “same app, lights off” of the three.

C · Open Water — cinema-first blue-black
Extends the video stage’s own deep ocean blue (#0e2230 in every mockup) into the whole canvas. The video melts into the UI — maximum cinematic immersion, the screen disappears around the footage. Coolest direction; warmth survives only in the taupe muted-text thread and the gold/coral semantics.
