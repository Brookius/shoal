Journal

Down the line to the M2

Thirty metres of green turned the shot line into a rope through nothing — and then the hull came out of the gloom all at once, the aircraft hangar door still open after ninety years. A conger thick as my thigh sat inside it, jaw open, entirely unbothered by us. Lobsters in every gap in the plating, a curled octopus tucked in under the gun mount, plumose anemones so dense the steel reads as soft. Thirty minutes wasn't enough. It never is on her.

Where it runs

How do I use Shoal?

Two ways, depending on what you dive with. There's no app store in either case — you install it straight from your browser, or download it.

Recommended

Android

Install it from Chrome or Edge in a few taps — this is the one to use in the field. Your dives sync straight to your own Google Drive folder, and the app updates itself quietly in the background. Pair a Bluetooth dive computer and pull your dives straight in.

Mac

A real desktop app — download it, no browser needed. The same Bluetooth dive-computer sync as Android, and a bigger screen for writing up a trip afterwards. Shoal tells you in-app when a new version's ready.

Download for Mac ↓

On iPhone it runs and logs dives offline, but backup is still manual — you'd need to export your dives yourself from Settings. Proper sync, like Android's, is being built.
