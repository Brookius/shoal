Where your dives live

You choose where your dives are kept — only one can be active at a time. In your browser stores them on this device only; works offline, but back up manually using Export. In a folder writes one .md file per dive to a folder you pick — choose a Google Drive folder for automatic cloud backup.

Pick any folder your device can see — a local folder, your Obsidian vault, or a Google Drive folder. Every save writes one .md file there automatically (your browser may ask permission once per session).

On your phone: when the picker opens, browse to Google Drive and choose or create a folder — you only pick once, then every dive backs up to the cloud.

Dive files

Every dive is a .md (Markdown) file — plain text that opens in any editor, imports into Obsidian, and will still be readable decades from now. No proprietary format, no lock-in.

Your data stays yours. Shoal doesn't store copies anywhere. Nothing leaves your device unless you choose a folder that syncs to a cloud service you control. Import and export let you move your history freely — between devices, into other apps, or as a personal archive. Re-importing is always safe: duplicates are skipped automatically.

Proton Drive: Files must be downloaded to your device first — Proton's end-to-end encryption means the browser can't read cloud-only files directly.

Unvalidated species

Marine life you've logged as free text — not matched against the built-in species database — is tracked separately. Export the list to research and add the most-logged ones to the database properly (scientific name, AphiaID, conservation status, reference photo).

Add profiles to dives you've already logged

Export a .uddf file from your dive computer's software (Dive Exporter, Subsurface, MacDive, or your vendor's app) and drop it in here to append the depth/time profile onto dives already in your history — matched by depth and bottom time, not the file's timestamp, since computer clocks drift. Anything uncertain shows up below for you to confirm.

Logging a new dive instead? Import from the Log a dive page — it'll start the entry pre-filled.

Match footage to dives

Reads each video's own capture time to work out which dive it belongs to — no need to sort your footage into per-dive folders first. Subfolders are searched too, so one trip folder is enough. Anything it can't place with confidence is listed for you rather than guessed at.

Your files aren't touched: nothing is moved, renamed or re-encoded — this only links them to dives inside Shoal, and the videos become playable in each dive's footage view. Works best when the camera's clock was set to local time before the dive; if it wasn't, you can correct the offset before confirming.

Bluetooth sync history

Dive computers you've synced remember where they left off, so routine syncs only fetch what's new. If a sync ever seems to be missing dives it shouldn't, forget the computer here to force a full re-sync next time.

About Shoal
Safety notice

Shoal is a logbook and a planning aid. It is not a dive computer, and it is not a substitute for proper training, your own planning, or the instruments you dive with.

The Plan panel's decompression figures come from a Bühlmann ZHL-16C model applied to a square-profile assumption — the same simplification printed dive tables make. Real dives are not square profiles, and no model accounts for your own physiology on the day. Treat every number Shoal shows as an estimate to sanity-check against your training and your computer, never as an instruction.

Always dive within the limits of your certification, follow the ascent rates and stops your own dive computer gives you, and plan conservatively. Shoal is provided without warranty of any kind; you dive at your own risk.
