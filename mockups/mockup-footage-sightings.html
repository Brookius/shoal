Decisions baked in — ready for sign-off:
  • Card per species, no clip count in header. The clips below are the count — no need to summarise what's visible.
  • Species name at --text-base (16px) — same weight as video filenames in the video panel. The two columns now feel balanced as a pair.
  • Filename first, timestamp second. Filename is the primary reference (which file to open). Timestamp is secondary context (where in that file). This mirrors the video panel's logic: video card is the heading, timestamps are what's inside it.
  • Blue removed from timestamps. Three alternatives shown in Section 2 — pick V1 (most differentiated), V2 (most unified), or V3 (dot-separator). V1 is the main design above.
  • Unlinked card has a subtle amber border tint — "needs attention" without being loud. Link button matches the vid-add dashed style.
  • Group emoji provides instant taxonomy scan (🦈 vs 🐢 vs 🐟) — same role as 🎬 in the video panel.
